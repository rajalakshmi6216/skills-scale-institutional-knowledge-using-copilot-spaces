# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository holds the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme to plan, deliver, measure, and improve cross-functional work. The documents in this folder provide a lightweight operating model for teams that need a shared process for intake, planning, execution, communication, quality assurance, and release management.

## Project management process overview

OctoAcme’s approach starts with initiation: validating the business need, identifying stakeholders, clarifying the desired outcome, and deciding whether the work should advance into planning. Once approved, teams move into backlog creation, prioritization, milestone planning, and definition of done so that work is broken into shippable increments with clear ownership and dependencies. That structure keeps the project focused on customer value and measurable results while enabling teams to manage scope and risk with greater visibility.

The execution model is grounded in clear roles and a regular communication rhythm. Product managers define outcomes and backlog priority, project managers coordinate schedules, risks, planning, and status communication, developers implement and validate the solution, and QA helps confirm that quality and acceptance criteria are met. Daily standups, weekly alignment meetings, demos, and milestone reviews ensure that progress, blockers, and cross-team dependencies are surfaced early and addressed quickly.

OctoAcme also places strong emphasis on risk management and release readiness. The team maintains a risk register, communicates stakeholder updates, and escalates blockers through structured paths when issues require product or leadership intervention. Quality practices include unit testing, integration testing, smoke testing for critical flows, security scanning, and manual QA when required. Before release, teams verify acceptance criteria, confirm CI status, document release notes, and prepare rollback or mitigation plans to reduce operational risk.

At the end of each sprint or milestone, teams conduct retrospectives to capture lessons learned, convert them into action items, and improve future delivery cycles. This continuous improvement mindset helps the organization learn, adapt, and sustain a repeatable project execution process over time.
