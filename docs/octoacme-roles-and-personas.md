# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads keep execution moving across the team by coordinating sprint work, resolving blockers, and aligning product, engineering, and delivery priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate sprint execution and ensure work is ready for each iteration
- Remove or escalate blockers that slow delivery
- Align engineering throughput with roadmap and milestone priorities
- Manage cross-team dependencies and sequencing of work
- Partner with Project Managers and developers to maintain delivery health

### Goals
- Keep the team focused on high-priority, shippable work
- Improve predictability and reduce delivery friction
- Strengthen collaboration across functional teams

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and dependency tracking
- Cross-functional planning and risk reviews
- Updates to PMs, product leads, and engineering leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on schedule, risks, and stakeholder communication
- Supports Product Managers by translating priorities into delivery-ready work
- Collaborates with Developers to identify bottlenecks and keep scope realistic

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure that work is designed and delivered in a way that meets policy, security, and risk requirements for the organization and its customers.

### Responsibilities
- Review solutions for security and compliance risks early in design
- Define required controls, review checkpoints, and policy alignment
- Partner with engineering on secure architecture, threat review, and remediation
- Support risk assessment for regulated or sensitive workstreams
- Escalate issues that require sponsor or leadership action

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Ensure decisions are informed by policy, risk, and control requirements
- Build trust with stakeholders and downstream teams

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and compliance checkpoints
- Risk and mitigation updates for PMs, product leads, and leadership
- Guidance during incidents or remediation planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and Product Managers to ensure security requirements are considered without blocking value delivery
- Coordinates with Project Managers on issue tracking, escalation paths, and risk visibility
- Supports Delivery Leads by identifying dependencies that may affect release readiness

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads help teams measure whether work is producing the intended outcomes by tracking product performance, success metrics, and operational signals.

### Responsibilities
- Define and validate success metrics and measurement plans
- Monitor product or service performance, adoption, quality, and customer value
- Translate operational data into business and product insights
- Partner with Product and PM teams to recommend iteration based on evidence
- Support dashboards, reporting, and post-release analysis

### Goals
- Improve decision-making with measurable evidence
- Clarify what success looks like for each milestone or feature
- Drive continuous learning and outcome-based iteration

### Typical Communication
- Metric reviews and analytics dashboards
- Stakeholder updates tied to business impact and adoption
- Performance summaries during planning, release, and retrospective cycles

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Product Managers on success criteria, outcomes, and feature validation
- Helps Project Managers communicate progress using objective metrics and risks
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure instrumentation and observability are in place

---

## Release Managers / Change Leads

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment readiness, communication, and post-release verification to reduce risk and support smooth go-live execution.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release timing, sequencing, and deployment readiness checks
- Coordinate with engineering, QA, and stakeholders on release criteria
- Own communication for deployment windows, status, and rollback actions
- Ensure rollback and incident mitigation plans are documented and ready
- Verify post-deployment health and support follow-up actions

### Goals
- Minimize release risk and operational disruption
- Improve confidence in deployment quality and stakeholder communication
- Create a repeatable release process for teams and projects

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Stakeholder updates before and after production changes
- Incident and rollback communications when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Developers and QA to confirm readiness and validation criteria
- Works with Project Managers on scheduling, dependencies, and communications
- Supports Product Managers by aligning release timing with customer and business priorities

---

## Executive Sponsors / Stakeholder Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic oversight, sponsorship, and decision support for initiatives that require executive alignment or escalation.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic priority and expected business outcomes
- Provide executive support, directions, and approvals when needed
- Help resolve cross-organization challenges or dependencies
- Escalate issues that require sponsor-level attention or resource decisions
- Maintain alignment between project delivery and business goals

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative remains aligned with broader organizational priorities
- Remove barriers that require executive sponsorship
- Support long-term accountability and strategic success

### Typical Communication
- Steering updates, milestone reviews, and governance checkpoints
- Escalation summaries for unresolved risk or business impact
- Sponsor-level alignment on priority and funding decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers and Project Managers on strategic alignment and escalation paths
- Provides guidance without replacing day-to-day delivery ownership
- Supports Security, Delivery, and Release leaders when critical business or policy decisions are needed

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles help clarify accountability, escalation paths, and collaboration for complex delivery work.
